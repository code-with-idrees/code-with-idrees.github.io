About

I am an undergraduate researcher at FAST-NUCES Islamabad working at the intersection of deep learning, high-performance computing, and generative AI. I am a Stanford Code in Place Section Leader '25 and was selected for the CERN Beamline for Schools (BL4S) program.

My work ranges from designing memory-efficient transformer architectures to building production-grade multi-agent LLM pipelines. I care about making models that actually work — fast, interpretable, and deployable. I write about my research on Medium.

I am actively seeking PhD positions in Machine Learning, NLP, or HPC for Fall 2026/2027. I am also open to research internships and collaborations.
